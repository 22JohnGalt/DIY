02 What we’re building

Small models, open tools, in your hands.

We experiment with small models and open tools that people can run, inspect, and adapt for themselves. Our interest is less in making AI bigger, and more in making it useful, understandable, and within reach.

Nothing here is a product announcement—these are the shapes we’re working toward, noted as we go.

  • N.01 Small models that fit ordinary hardware.
  • N.02 Open tools you can read, run, and modify.
  • N.03 Experiments documented in the open.

03 Principles

Interests, not promises.

What guides the lab—stated plainly, with nothing guaranteed.

  1. P.01

    Small by design

    Useful systems should not always require vast infrastructure.

  2. P.02

    Open to tinker with

    Free and open-source tools make learning and adaptation possible.

  3. P.03

    Run it yourself

    Self-hosting can give people more agency over how tools fit into their lives.

  4. P.04

    Built through experiments

    We learn by making, testing, documenting, and revising.

04 The workshop

How the work unfolds.

A plain loop, repeated—kept honest by writing things down.

  1. Step 01

    Collect

    Papers, notebooks, and weights that fit on a laptop.

  2. Step 02

    Build

    Small prototypes, tuned by hand and measured against real needs.

  3. Step 03

    Test

    On our own machines, with honest notes—including what fails.

  4. Step 04

    Share

    Documented so a friend can repeat it, improve it, and pass it on.
