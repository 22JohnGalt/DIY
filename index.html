Make intelligence your own.

DAIY Lab is an independent research lab for small, self-hostable AI: free, open-source models and tools that run on your own hardware.

Explore the lab Read the principles

Small models, open tools, your hardware.

We experiment with small models and open tools that people can run, inspect, and adapt for themselves. Our interest is less in making AI bigger, and more in making it useful, understandable, and within reach.

The work is practical: prototype, measure on ordinary hardware, document what holds up and what doesn’t, and publish the pieces others can build on.

What guides the work

Guiding interests for the lab, stated plainly with nothing guaranteed.

  • Small by design

    Capability should not require a data center. Small models keep AI affordable, inspectable, and close at hand.

  • Open by license

    Free and open-source code and weights, so anyone can read them, verify them, and change them.

  • Private by default

    Self-hosting is the point: your documents, your queries, and your outputs stay on your machine.

  • Evidence over promise

    We measure on ordinary hardware, document what we find, and say plainly when something doesn’t work.

How the work moves

A plain loop, repeated, kept honest by documentation.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Papers, open datasets, and checkpoints small enough to run on one machine.

  2. 02

    Build

    Prototypes assembled from open components and tuned by hand.

  3. 03

    Test

    Benchmarks on ordinary hardware, including the numbers that disappoint.

  4. 04

    Publish

    Code, models, and results under open licenses, so others can replicate them.

If you work on small models, self-hosting, or open tooling, we’d like to compare notes.
